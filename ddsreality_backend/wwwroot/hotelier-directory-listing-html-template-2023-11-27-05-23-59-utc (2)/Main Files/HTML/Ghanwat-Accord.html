Opposite Ghanwat Estate (Royal Pristo), Vaishey Pada, Malad East        

Ghanwat Accord brings you a thoughtfully designed collection of 1 BHK and 2 BHK residences that combine style, comfort, and smart living. Located in a prime area with excellent connectivity, these homes offer the perfect balance of convenience and tranquility. With modern features like a lounge-inspired lobby, a state-of-the-art gymnasium, and elegant lift lobbies, every detail has been created to elevate your lifestyle.
