Vivan

Vivan

Plot No. CTS No 92 to 97, Ram Mandir Road, Oshiwara District Centre, Goregaon (W), Mumbai – 400104

Vivan is a thoughtfully designed residential development that blends modern comfort with the warmth of community living. Located in Oshiwara District Centre, Goregaon West – often referred to as the "Next BKC" – the project offers premium 1BHK and 2BHK residences with well-planned layouts, elegant finishes, and sustainable features. Designed for modern families, Vivan provides the perfect balance between privacy and a connected lifestyle.

  • Premium 1BHK & 2BHK residences with smart layouts
  • Designed for community living with modern comforts
  • Prime location in ODC, Goregaon (W) with excellent connectivity
  • Lifestyle amenities for leisure, fitness, and social bonding
  • 24x7 security, fire-safety systems & DG backup for common areas
  • EV charging provisions and 2-level basement parking
  • Sustainable features like water recycling & organic waste management

Every home at Vivan is built with superior specifications, including premium vitrified flooring, granite kitchen platforms with stainless steel sinks, full-height French windows for natural light and ventilation, branded sanitary fittings, waterproof doors, and video door phone intercoms for enhanced safety. The project also emphasizes eco-friendly living through water recycling, organic waste management, and provisions for piped gas connections. With its strategic location, Vivan offers unmatched connectivity. Ram Mandir Station is just a minute away, while malls, schools, hospitals, and business hubs like Inorbit Mall, Infiniti Mall, Goregaon Sports Club, Vibgyor International School, and the International Airport are all within easy reach. This makes Vivan not only a comfortable home but also a smart investment in one of Mumbai’s most promising hubs.mbai.
