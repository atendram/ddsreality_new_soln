Sai Radha

Sai Radha

Julius Wadi CHS Ltd., Jay Prakash Nagar, Road No 5, Goregaon East, Mumbai - 400063

Sai Radha offers premium residential apartments that redefine urban living with elegance, comfort, and modern design. Nestled in the heart of Goregaon East on Aarey Road, the project is thoughtfully crafted to balance contemporary lifestyles with natural greenery. With excellent connectivity to schools, hospitals, shopping hubs, and business districts, Sai Radha is the perfect blend of convenience and tranquility.

  • Premium residences with modern layouts
  • Elegant architecture with natural surroundings
  • Prime location with excellent connectivity
  • Lifestyle amenities for leisure and fitness
  • Clubhouse and landscaped gardens
  • Elevators and ample parking facilities
  • 24x7 Security & Power Backup

The development is enriched with lifestyle features such as a grand entrance lobby, landscaped gardens, children’s play area, clubhouse, and a well-equipped fitness zone. Designed to offer both privacy and community living, Sai Radha ensures that residents enjoy peace of mind while staying connected to the vibrant city life. With a commitment to trust, transparency, and quality, Sai Radha Goregaon East stands as a proud symbol of modern living in Mumbai.
